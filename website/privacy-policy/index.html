At Culerson, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how we collect, use, and share your information. This Privacy Policy explains the types of data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal information.

1. Who We Are

Culerson is a specialized energy consulting firm providing data-driven intelligence to support energy investment, infrastructure planning, and regulatory navigation. Our website address is: https://culerson.com.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide to Us

We may collect personal data when you:

  • Subscribe to our newsletter or download reports.
  • Contact us via forms or email.
  • Register for an account or leave a comment.

This data may include:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and company affiliation.
  • Any other details you provide voluntarily.

B. Automated Data Collection

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • Device and browser data: IP address, browser type, and operating system.
  • Usage data: Pages visited, time spent, and interactions with our content.
  • Cookies and tracking technologies: To enhance user experience and analyze site performance.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies to improve your experience on our website. Cookies help store your preferences and recognize returning visitors. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic site functionality.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand visitor behavior and improve our services.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used for targeted advertising and social media features.

To opt out of cookies, adjust your browser settings or use tools like Google’s Ads Settings.

4. Embedded Content & Third-Party Links

Our website may include embedded content (e.g., videos, articles, images) from external sources such as YouTube, LinkedIn, or industry publications. These third-party websites may collect data, use cookies, or track your interactions as if you visited their website directly.

We are not responsible for the privacy policies of third-party websites, and we encourage you to review their policies.

5. Who We Share Your Data With

Culerson does not sell or rent your data. However, we may share information with:

  • Service providers & partners: To support our website, analytics, or marketing efforts.
  • Regulatory authorities: If required by law or to protect our legal rights.

If you request a password reset, your IP address will be included in the reset email.

6. How Long We Retain Your Data

  • Comments & Form Submissions: Retained indefinitely to improve engagement and responses.
  • User Accounts: If you register, we store your data until you request deletion.
  • Analytics & Cookies: Retention varies, typically ranging from 30 days to 2 years.

You can request the deletion of your data by contacting us. However, certain legal or security obligations may require us to retain some information.

7. Your Rights Over Your Data

You have the right to:
✅ Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
✅ Request corrections or updates to your information.
✅ Request that we erase your data (except where required by law).
✅ Opt out of marketing communications at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact info@culerson.com.

8. Where Your Data Is Sent

To protect our website from spam, visitor comments may be checked through an automated spam detection service. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Updates to This Policy

Culerson may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected on this page, and significant updates will be communicated via email or website notification.
