Making renewable energy work in practice

Culerson helps businesses, developers and public agencies make decisions about how they generate, store and use power.

Our story

Culerson was founded in 2024 to help organizations modernize their energy systems and meet sustainability goals.

We’re a minority-owned consultancy based in Houston. We help teams assess their options, plan renewable projects and improve the systems they already operate.

What guides our work

  • Diversity and inclusion

    We support equitable opportunities in the energy sector.

  • Innovation

    We evaluate new technologies against the problem they need to solve.

  • Sustainability

    We account for how energy decisions affect the environment and the communities that rely on them.

  • Technical rigor

    We examine the data, test assumptions and explain the tradeoffs.
